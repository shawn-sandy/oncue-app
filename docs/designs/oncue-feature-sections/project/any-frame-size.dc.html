Any frame

Vertical for Shorts. Widescreen for YouTube.

One format line sets the size. Frames wider than tall get the landscape layout, the rest get the portrait one, and the same spec renders either way.

One line in the spec 

"format": { "width": 1920, "height": 1080 }

Portrait layouts keep content out of the bottom of the frame, clear of the Shorts buttons and captions.

Shrunk, never cut off

A scene with more than fits its frame is scaled down to fit. You get a warning naming it, and every scene’s scale is recorded in build/fit.json.

The deck follows

The deck is drawn at 9:16 or 16:9. Landscape specs get 16:9, portrait and square ones 9:16, and the video keeps the exact size.
