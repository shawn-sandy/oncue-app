Nothing made up

Real facts. Real pictures. Clear gaps.

Point Claude at a repo, a README, a docs page or your notes. It uses real facts only, and marks anything missing as [YOUR FACT] for you to fill in.

Research

Writes research.md

Joins at any stage, from a bare topic to a rendered video, and works out where you are from the files in the folder.

  • 3 to 5 ranked topic ideas, each with a hook line, an audience and sources
  • A fact-check of the storyboard
  • Narration rewrites, shown before and after, with sources
  • Icon and picture ideas for each beat

Once a take is recorded, it changes no line without asking, and names the re-record cost first.

Visuals

Adds image beats

Every picture gets alt text and a cue word. Pictures change only the scene, so they are safe to add after the voiceover.

  • Places your screenshots and photos
  • Draws diagrams, charts and timelines
  • Uses an image-generation tool only when your session has one
  • Finds openly licensed images, with credits

Never drawn or generated

  • A product’s interface
  • A real person
  • A logo
  • A chart of real data

Asked for one with nothing supplied, it asks for the real picture or offers a labelled diagram.
