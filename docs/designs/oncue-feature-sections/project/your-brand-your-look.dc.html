Same beat, two themes

No brand to hand?

Claude uses the product’s logo and site. With neither, it picks a dark ground, one accent and one highlight from the subject, and tells you.

Contrast is checked

The check command warns when a text colour and its fill fall under 4.5:1, and names both keys.

Restyle any time
