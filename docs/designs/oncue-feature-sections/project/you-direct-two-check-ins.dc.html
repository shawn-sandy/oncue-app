You direct

You approve the beats and the voice.

Claude writes the storyboard, records the voiceover and renders the video. It stops twice on the way, so you can steer before anything is recorded or rendered.

  1. Step 1

    Storyboard

    Claude writes the beats into short.json.

  2. Check-in 1

    The beats

    See the beat list with preview stills. Change, cut or reorder beats now: once the voiceover is recorded, a wording change means paying for a new recording.

  3. Step 2

    Voiceover

    ElevenLabs reads the whole script in one take, then transcribes it with word timestamps.

  4. Check-in 2

    The voice

    Claude names the voice it picked and gives you two takes. Pick one, or ask for another voice.

  5. Step 3

    Render

    Claude builds the deck if you asked for one, checks stills against the real word timings, and renders the video.

At the end you get

  • The finished MP4
  • The deck, if you asked for one
  • The take and the voice used
  • The ElevenLabs credits spent

In a hurry?

Say “go ahead without stopping” to skip both check-ins.

For scale: a 75-second script cost about 1,100 ElevenLabs credits per take, and about 600 to transcribe. That is why the beats come first.
