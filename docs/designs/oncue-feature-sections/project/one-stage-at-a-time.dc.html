Eight skills

Run the whole pipeline, or only the stage you need.

Each stage is its own skill, and you call it in plain words. Stop after any of them, or start from a short.json you already have.

The whole pipeline

explainer-video

“Make an explainer short about our CLI”

Storyboard to finished video, with check-ins at the beats and the voice.

  • setup

    “Set up OnCue”

    Checks Node, ffmpeg, Playwright and ElevenLabs, installs the engine, and says which outputs are ready.

  • research

    “Suggest three Shorts about this tool”

    A research.md with ranked topics, sourced facts and visual ideas.

  • storyboard

    “Storyboard a 60-second Short for this plugin”

    A short.json, a beat list, and preview stills or a Design canvas.

  • visuals

    “Add a diagram of how it works to beat 3”

    Image beats with alt text, a cue word and credits. Safe after the voiceover.

  • voiceover

    “Voice it with ElevenLabs”

    Two takes as MP3, each with a word-level transcript.

  • vertical-deck

    “Make the 9:16 slides”

    An editable .pptx with the voiceover in the speaker notes.

  • animate-short

    “Render it, and animate the slides”

    The final MP4, or a silent preview before any audio exists.
