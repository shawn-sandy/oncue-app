Word-synced animation

Every card lands on the word that names it.

OnCue times the animation from a word-level transcript, not from the audio. Each element names its cue word, and it enters the moment the narrator says it.

Also in every frame

  • A typing effect
  • Spotlight glows
  • Drawn lines
  • Scene transitions
  • A progress bar

If the narrator never says a cue word, the build tells you, and that element is spaced evenly instead of landing on its word.
